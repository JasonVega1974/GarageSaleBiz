The business, in four steps

You are the person who
makes the sale happen.

No inventory, no storefront, no van full of stock. The goods belong to the homeowner until they sell, and you get paid out of what they sell for.

1

Find the sale

People moving, downsizing, clearing a parent's garage. Module 1 covers the five places these jobs come from and the exact words that get you the appointment.

2

Sign the agreement

Generate a plain-language agreement in the dashboard — your commission, the dates, who pays for what. It prints or saves as a PDF. Signed before you lift a single box.

3

Price, stage, advertise

Photograph the good pieces onto your own website, print arrow signs for every corner within half a mile, and post to the local groups.

4

Run it and settle up

Work the sale, mark items sold as they go, drop prices on the last day, hand the homeowner their share, and keep yours.

You set your own rate. Operators in this trade commonly charge somewhere between 40% and 60% of what a sale takes in, depending on how much of the work is theirs — a full clear-out where you price, stage, staff, and clean up is worth more than a Saturday where the homeowner does half of it. The dashboard defaults to 50% and you change it per job. We do not set your prices and we make no promise about what you will earn.
